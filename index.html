About this project

I bought an old Olympus E-300, and despite all of its quirks, it made me enjoy photography more than I expected. This project is just my way of staying consistent, sharing five photos each week, and seeing where the journey takes me.

My goal is to keep photographing. Sharing five photos each week gives me a reason to keep picking up my camera and continue improving, five photos at a time.

Thanks for showing up! — Sean
